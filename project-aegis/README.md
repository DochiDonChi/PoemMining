# Project Aegis: Trustworthy AI Investment Research

Project Aegis is an independent research workspace for studying **LLM-based financial agents** and building a path toward **trustworthy AI investment research systems**.

## Core thesis

The most valuable research direction is not simply "LLM stock prediction" or "AI trading bot". The stronger PhD-level direction is:

> **Reproducible, auditable, risk-aware multi-agent systems for autonomous investment research.**

This means studying how AI agents can support the full buy-side research workflow: hypothesis generation, evidence retrieval, financial reasoning, risk assessment, portfolio implication, human review, and audit trail.

## Why this matters

Recent papers show that LLM financial agents are moving from isolated prediction tasks toward agentic decision pipelines. However, the field still has weak standards for evaluation, transaction costs, reproducibility, execution semantics, auditability, and governance.

A 2026 survey, *Agentic Trading: When LLM Agents Meet Financial Markets*, reviewed 77 studies and found major protocol incomparability. In its core empirical subset, only a small number of papers reported time-consistent split protocols, explicit transaction-cost models, survivorship handling, or high reproducibility standards. This supports the claim that the next bottleneck is **trust**, not just model architecture.
