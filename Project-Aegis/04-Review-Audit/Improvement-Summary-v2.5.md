# Improvement Summary v2.5

## Date

2026-07-09

## Purpose

This iteration follows the recommendation from `Improvement-Summary-v2.4.md` by rewriting the living review draft using the current three-anchor structure.

The goal is to convert extraction work into a coherent literature-review narrative.

## Improvements completed

### 1. Living Review Draft v0.2 added

Added:

`Living-Review-Draft-v0.2.md`

This draft replaces the older broad v0.1 narrative with a sharper three-anchor structure:

| Anchor | Role |
|---|---|
| P001 Agentic Trading | reproducibility and protocol-reporting gap anchor |
| P016 FinRobot | equity research workflow / valuation-system anchor |
| P025 FinRpt | dataset / benchmark / report-generation anchor |

### 2. Review framing improved

The new draft frames the field as splitting into two streams:

1. trading-action agents;
2. research-artifact agents.

This is important because Project Aegis is not simply about autonomous trading. It is about trustworthy AI investment research artifacts.

### 3. Project Aegis thesis clarified

The new draft states the bridge thesis more clearly:

> Apply the reproducibility and protocol discipline learned from trading-agent research to emerging investment research agents, where the key output is an auditable research artifact rather than a market action.

### 4. Evidence status clarified

The draft separates:

- strongly supported claims;
- moderately supported claims;
- not-yet-supported claims.

This prevents overclaiming.

## What became stronger

### Stronger synthesis

The project now has a narrative that connects methodology, extraction, and research gap.

### Better thesis identity

Project Aegis is now more clearly positioned as a research agenda for:

> reproducible, evidence-grounded, risk-aware, human-reviewable investment research agents.

### Better use of P001/P016/P025

The draft no longer treats all financial-agent papers equally. It uses:

- P001 for reproducibility warning;
- P016 for valuation workflow and deterministic compute;
- P025 for dataset/benchmark/report-generation evidence.

## What remains weak

1. The draft still lacks formal citations and line-level source references.
2. Full Search-Round-02 remains incomplete.
3. PRISMA counts remain provisional.
4. P001 exact R0-R3 definitions remain pending.
5. P016 and P025 claim-level evidence grounding remains incomplete.
6. Claim-Evidence Ledger has not yet been updated to reflect the new v0.2 thesis.

## Current status after v2.5

Project Aegis remains:

**Level 2.0 candidate / 5**

But the project is now stronger because it has moved from scattered evidence files toward a coherent living review draft.
