## Key methodological observation

Search-Round-01 confirms that the field is splitting into several related but distinct clusters:

1. **Agentic trading systems** — e.g. TradingAgents, HedgeAgents, Agentic Trading survey.
2. **Investment research agents** — e.g. Finance Agent Benchmark, FinRobot Equity Research.
3. **Financial LLM infrastructure** — e.g. BloombergGPT, FinGPT.
4. **Benchmark and evaluation platforms** — e.g. Agent Market Arena, Evaluation and Benchmarking Suite.
5. **General LLM agent theory** — e.g. general LLM agent surveys.
6. **Governance and systemic-risk literature** — e.g. regulatory reports and FCA/ESMA policy materials.

## Implication for Project Aegis

The strongest research gap is not merely building another financial agent. The gap is building a **trustworthy investment research agent framework** with reproducible methodology, benchmark discipline, auditability, risk-first evaluation, and human review.
