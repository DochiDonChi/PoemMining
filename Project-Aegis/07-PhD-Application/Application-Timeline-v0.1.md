# Application Timeline v0.1

## Goal

Use Project Aegis as a research portfolio for PhD applications.

## Phase 1 — Research portfolio strengthening

Target: 0-2 months

Tasks:

- finish Living Review Draft v0.2 improvement;
- update Claim-Evidence Ledger;
- verify P016 and P025 further;
- prepare one-page research brief;
- prepare academic CV;
- prepare short proposal.

## Phase 2 — Supervisor database

Target: 1-3 months

Tasks:

- identify 30 possible supervisors;
- verify official profiles and emails;
- score fit 0-100;
- shortlist top 10-15;
- read 2-3 recent papers per top supervisor.

## Phase 3 — Outreach

Target: 2-5 months

Tasks:

- send tailored emails;
- attach CV and short proposal;
- include Project Aegis GitHub link;
- record responses in contact log;
- schedule meetings.

## Phase 4 — Formal application

Target: 4-9 months

Tasks:

- finalize proposal;
- request recommendation letters;
- prepare transcripts and certificates;
- check English requirement;
- submit university and scholarship applications.

## Phase 5 — Interview and funding

Target: 6-12 months

Tasks:

- prepare supervisor meeting script;
- prepare PhD interview answers;
- refine proposal after feedback;
- confirm funding path.
