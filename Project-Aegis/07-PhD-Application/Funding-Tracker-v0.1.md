# Funding Tracker v0.1

## Purpose

Track PhD funding options, scholarship deadlines, supervisor funding, and application requirements.

## Funding categories

| Category | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| University scholarship | central PhD scholarship or postgraduate studentship | deadline-sensitive |
| Supervisor grant | funded by supervisor research project | depends on supervisor |
| Department studentship | department-level funding | often linked to nomination |
| External scholarship | government, foundation, industry, or country-specific funding | separate deadlines |
| Self-funded | not preferred | use only as fallback |
