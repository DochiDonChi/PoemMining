# Supervisor Cold Email Template v0.1

## Subject options

- Prospective PhD Student — Trustworthy AI Investment Research
- PhD Inquiry: Auditable AI Investment Research Agents
- Prospective PhD Research on LLM Agents in Finance

## Email template

Dear Professor [Surname],

I hope you are well. My name is [Name]. I am preparing a PhD proposal on trustworthy AI investment research, focusing on how LLM-based financial agents can generate investment research artifacts that are evidence-grounded, reproducible, auditable, and human-reviewable.

I have started a structured research project called Project Aegis. The current work reviews LLM-based financial agents and focuses on three anchors: reproducibility gaps in trading-agent research, equity research workflow agents, and equity research report generation benchmarks.

I am particularly interested in your work on [specific paper / research area], especially [one specific connection]. I believe there may be a good fit with my proposed research direction.

Would you be open to considering a PhD applicant working on trustworthy AI investment research agents? I would be grateful for the chance to send you a short proposal and CV, or to have a brief discussion if convenient.

Best regards,
[Name]
[Email]
[GitHub / Project Aegis link]
[CV link if available]

## Rule

Customize at least three lines before sending:

1. professor research area;
2. one specific paper;
3. why Project Aegis connects to their work.
