# Research Proposal Outline v0.1

## Working title

**Towards Trustworthy AI Investment Research: Reproducible, Auditable, Evidence-Grounded Investment Research Agents**

## 1. Background

LLM-based financial agents are moving from prediction and trading toward research artifact generation.

## 2. Research gap

Current systems can generate financial reports, but claim-level evidence grounding, reproducibility, auditability, and human review remain underdeveloped.

## 3. Research question

How can AI investment research agents generate research artifacts that are evidence-grounded, reproducible, risk-aware, and human-reviewable?

## 4. Literature anchors

| Anchor | Role |
|---|---|
| P001 Agentic Trading | reproducibility gap and protocol discipline |
| P016 FinRobot | equity research workflow and valuation architecture |
| P025 FinRpt | ERR dataset and report generation benchmark |
| P013 Finance Agent Benchmark | finance research task benchmark |
| P014 Evaluation Suite | governance and evaluation lifecycle |

## 5. Proposed contribution

1. Define investment research agent category.
2. Build evaluation framework for AI-generated investment research artifacts.
3. Propose auditability checklist.
4. Build prototype or case study.
5. Evaluate source grounding, reproducibility, and human usefulness.

## 6. Methodology

- systematic / living literature review;
- taxonomy coding;
- benchmark design;
- prototype workflow;
- case study on equity research report generation;
- human review or expert evaluation if feasible.

## 7. Expected outputs

- literature review paper;
- research framework paper;
- prototype or benchmark;
- PhD dissertation chapters.
