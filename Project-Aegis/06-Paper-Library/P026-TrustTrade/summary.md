# P026 Summary — TrustTrade

## Paper

**TrustTrade: Human-Inspired Selective Consensus Reduces Decision Uncertainty in LLM Trading Agents**

## Why this paper matters

P026 matters because it focuses on trust, selective consensus, misinformation, and uncertainty in LLM trading agents. This is useful for Project Aegis because trust is one of the core concerns in financial-agent design.

## One-sentence summary

P026 proposes a trust-oriented trading-agent approach that uses selective consensus to reduce uncertainty and improve decision behavior.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. What selective consensus means.
2. Why uniform trust can be dangerous.
3. How misinformation can affect LLM trading agents.
4. Why uncertainty matters in financial decision-making.
5. How trust mechanisms might inspire investment research agents.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P026 as a candidate paper for trust-aware and risk-aware financial-agent design.

Do **not** use P026 as proof that trustworthy investment research agents are solved. It is still primarily a trading-agent paper.

## Reading priority

Medium. Read after P024 if you want to understand trust and uncertainty in trading agents.
