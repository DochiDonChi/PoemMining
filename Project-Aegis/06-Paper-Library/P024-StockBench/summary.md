# P024 Summary — StockBench

## Paper

**StockBench: Can LLM Agents Trade Stocks Profitably In Real-world Markets?**

## Why this paper matters

P024 matters because it focuses on benchmarking LLM agents in realistic multi-month stock trading settings. It helps Project Aegis understand how trading-agent evaluation is moving toward more dynamic and contamination-aware benchmarks.

## One-sentence summary

P024 introduces StockBench, a benchmark designed to evaluate whether LLM agents can trade stocks profitably in realistic market environments.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. What StockBench is.
2. Why contamination-free evaluation matters.
3. Why multi-month trading benchmarks are harder than static tasks.
4. Why trading metrics such as return, drawdown, and Sortino matter.
5. How trading benchmarks differ from investment research benchmarks.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P024 as a trading benchmark candidate.

Do **not** use P024 as evidence that investment research agents are reliable. It evaluates trading performance, not research artifact quality.

## Reading priority

Medium. Read after P022 if you want to understand dynamic trading-agent benchmarks.
