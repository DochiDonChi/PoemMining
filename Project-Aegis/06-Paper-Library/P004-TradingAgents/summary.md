# P004 Summary — TradingAgents

## Paper

**TradingAgents: Multi-Agents LLM Financial Trading Framework**

## Why this paper matters

P004 matters because it is a clear example of a role-specialized multi-agent trading framework. It shows how financial agents can imitate parts of a trading firm by assigning different roles to analysts, researchers, traders, and risk managers.

## One-sentence summary

P004 proposes a multi-agent LLM trading framework where different agents play specialized financial roles and collaborate to produce trading decisions.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. What a multi-agent trading framework is.
2. Why role specialization matters.
3. Why bull/bear debate may be useful.
4. Why trading performance is not enough to prove trustworthiness.
5. How P004 differs from investment research agent papers like P016 and P025.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P004 as a baseline for multi-agent trading architecture.

Do **not** use P004 as direct proof that investment research agents are mature. Its primary output is trading action, not research artifact.

## Reading priority

Medium-high. Read after P013 and P014 if you want to understand multi-agent financial workflow design.
