# P016 Summary — FinRobot Equity Research

## Paper

**FinRobot: AI Agent for Equity Research and Valuation with Large Language Models**

## Why this paper matters

P016 is one of the first strong anchors for Project Aegis because it focuses on equity research and valuation rather than only trading decisions. This makes it directly relevant to the idea of an investment research agent.

## One-sentence summary

P016 proposes an equity research agent framework that uses specialized reasoning agents to gather data, reason about financial concepts, and produce investment thesis / research report style outputs.

## Key concepts

The paper is important because of its agent structure:

1. **Data-CoT Agent** — focuses on gathering and integrating financial data.
2. **Concept-CoT Agent** — focuses on financial reasoning and analyst-style insight generation.
3. **Thesis-CoT Agent** — focuses on synthesizing findings into an investment thesis or research report.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. Why equity research is different from trading.
2. What Data-CoT, Concept-CoT, and Thesis-CoT mean.
3. How an AI system can imitate parts of an analyst workflow.
4. Why generating a report is not the same as being trustworthy.
5. What evaluation is still missing before such a system can be used institutionally.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P016 to support the claim that equity research agents are emerging as a distinct category.

Do **not** use P016 to claim that investment research agents are already reliable, auditable, or institutionally ready.

## Reading priority

Very high. Read after P001 and before P025.
