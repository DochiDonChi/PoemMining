# P001 Summary — Agentic Trading

## Paper

**Agentic Trading: When LLM Agents Meet Financial Markets**

## Why this paper comes first

This is the best paper to start with because it gives Project Aegis a field-level map. Instead of studying one trading agent, it reviews the broader LLM trading-agent literature and identifies major weaknesses in evaluation and reproducibility.

## One-sentence summary

P001 argues that current LLM trading-agent research is promising but methodologically weak because studies often use inconsistent protocols, unclear trading assumptions, limited transaction-cost modeling, and poor reproducibility standards.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. What an LLM trading agent is.
2. Why trading-agent evaluation is difficult.
3. Why transaction costs, execution timing, and survivorship bias matter.
4. Why many reported trading results may not be comparable.
5. Why Project Aegis should focus on reproducibility and auditability rather than only performance.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P001 to support the reproducibility gap.

Do **not** use P001 alone to prove that all investment research agents are weak. P001 mainly supports claims about trading-agent literature.

## Reading priority

Very high. Read before P004, P006, P016, and P025.
