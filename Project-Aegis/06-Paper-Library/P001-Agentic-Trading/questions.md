# P001 Reading Questions

## Basic understanding

1. What problem is this paper trying to solve?
2. Is this paper a system paper, benchmark paper, or review paper?
3. What kinds of LLM trading agents does it cover?
4. What does the paper mean by reproducibility?
5. Why is protocol comparability important?

## Methodology questions

1. How many papers or studies are included?
2. How does the paper define its primary empirical subset?
3. What are the inclusion and exclusion criteria?
4. What coding dimensions does the paper use?
5. Does the paper follow PRISMA or another evidence-map method?

## Finance questions

1. Why do transaction costs matter in trading-agent evaluation?
2. Why does execution timing matter?
3. What is survivorship bias?
4. Why is universe definition important?
5. What is the difference between return, Sharpe ratio, drawdown, turnover, and transaction-cost-aware performance?

## Reviewer questions

1. Does this paper prove that LLM trading agents do not work?
2. Does it prove that investment research agents are underdeveloped?
3. Which claims from this paper are strong enough to cite?
4. Which claims are limited to trading-agent studies?
5. How should Project Aegis extend this paper without overclaiming?

## Project Aegis answer target

After reading P001, you should be able to say:

> P001 shows that LLM trading-agent research has serious reproducibility and evaluation problems. Project Aegis extends this concern from trading agents toward investment research agents, where auditability, evidence grounding, and human review are also critical.
