# P013 Summary — Finance Agent Benchmark

## Paper

**Finance Agent Benchmark: Benchmarking LLMs on Real-world Financial Research Tasks**

## Why this paper matters

P013 matters because it focuses on real-world financial research tasks, not only trading decisions or generic financial question answering. It helps Project Aegis understand how difficult professional finance research tasks remain for current LLM agents.

## One-sentence summary

P013 introduces a benchmark of expert-authored financial research questions based on SEC filings and tests how well LLM agents can solve realistic financial research tasks.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. Why real-world financial research tasks are difficult.
2. Why SEC filings are useful but challenging sources.
3. Why benchmark design matters for financial agents.
4. Why strong general LLMs may still struggle with finance research.
5. How this paper supports the Project Aegis claim that finance research agents need better evaluation.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P013 to support the claim that current LLM agents still struggle with expert-level finance research tasks.

Do **not** use P013 as proof that all investment research systems are impossible or unreliable. It is a benchmark paper, not a complete investment research system paper.

## Reading priority

High. Read after P001, P016, and P025.
