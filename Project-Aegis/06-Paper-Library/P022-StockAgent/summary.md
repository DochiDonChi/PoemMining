# P022 Summary — StockAgent

## Paper

**When AI Meets Finance (StockAgent): Large Language Model-based Stock Trading in Simulated Real-world Environments**

## Why this paper matters

P022 matters because it studies LLM-based stock trading in simulated real-world environments. It helps Project Aegis understand how trading-agent papers try to create more realistic market simulations and avoid overly clean benchmark settings.

## One-sentence summary

P022 proposes StockAgent, an LLM-based stock trading simulation framework designed to evaluate agent behavior in more realistic market-like environments.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. What StockAgent is.
2. Why simulated real-world environments matter.
3. Why test-set leakage is a concern in financial AI.
4. How trading simulation differs from investment research generation.
5. How P022 helps Project Aegis understand trading-agent evaluation.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P022 as a trading-agent simulation candidate.

Do **not** use P022 as evidence that investment research agents are mature. Its main focus is trading simulation, not research artifact generation.

## Reading priority

Medium. Read after the first seven core papers if you want to understand trading-agent simulation and benchmark design.
