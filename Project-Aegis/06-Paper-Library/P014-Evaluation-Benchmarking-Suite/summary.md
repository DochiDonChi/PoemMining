# P014 Summary — Evaluation and Benchmarking Suite

## Paper

**Evaluation and Benchmarking Suite for Financial Large Language Models and Agents**

## Why this paper matters

P014 matters because it frames financial LLM and agent evaluation as a lifecycle and governance problem, not only a model-performance problem. This is closely aligned with Project Aegis, which focuses on trustworthy AI investment research.

## One-sentence summary

P014 proposes an evaluation and benchmarking suite for financial LLMs and agents, covering evaluation pipelines, governance, leaderboards, AgentOps, and documentation.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. Why financial AI evaluation needs more than accuracy.
2. What lifecycle evaluation means.
3. Why governance matters for financial LLMs and agents.
4. What a leaderboard can and cannot prove.
5. How AgentOps connects to reliable deployment.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P014 to support the claim that financial AI evaluation is moving toward lifecycle evaluation and governance.

Do **not** use P014 as proof that investment research agents are already fully evaluated or institutionally ready.

High. Read after P013.
