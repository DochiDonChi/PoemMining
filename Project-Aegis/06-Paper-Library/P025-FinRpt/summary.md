# P025 Summary — FinRpt

## Paper

**FinRpt: Dataset, Evaluation System and LLM-based Multi-agent Framework for Equity Research Report Generation**

## Why this paper matters

P025 is one of the most important new papers surfaced in Search-Round-02A. It directly targets equity research report generation, which is close to Project Aegis's core interest: trustworthy investment research agents.

## One-sentence summary

P025 studies how LLM-based multi-agent systems can generate equity research reports and how those reports can be evaluated with a dataset and evaluation system.

## Why it is important for Project Aegis

Before P025, P016 was the main equity research agent anchor. P025 gives the project a second strong anchor because it focuses on the output that matters for research agents: the research report.

## What you should understand after reading it

After reading this paper, you should be able to explain:

1. What equity research report generation means.
2. What dataset the paper introduces.
3. What evaluation metrics are used.
4. How the multi-agent framework works.
5. Whether the report claims are grounded in evidence.
6. Whether the system is useful for analysts or only an academic demo.

## Key Project Aegis use

Use P025 to support the claim that equity research report generation is emerging as an important subproblem for investment research agents.

Do **not** use P025 to claim that AI-generated research reports are already reliable, auditable, or institutionally ready until full-text extraction is completed.

## Reading priority

Very high. Read after P001 and P016.
