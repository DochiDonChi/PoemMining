5. Is there a mechanism for reviewer correction?

## Project Aegis answer target

After reading P025, you should be able to say:

> P025 strengthens the investment research agent category because it focuses on equity research report generation, dataset construction, evaluation, and multi-agent workflow. However, its usefulness for Project Aegis depends on whether the generated reports are evidence-grounded, auditable, and useful for human investment decision-making.
