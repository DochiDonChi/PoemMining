# Question Bank v0.1

This document records questions that a supervisor, reviewer, industry expert, or PhD committee may ask about Project Aegis.

## A. Supervisor questions

1. What is the exact research question?
2. Why is this a PhD-level problem rather than an engineering project?
3. What is new compared with existing LLM trading-agent papers?
4. Why focus on investment research agents instead of trading agents?
5. Why is trust the core bottleneck?
6. What evidence supports the claim that reproducibility is weak in the field?
7. What evidence supports the claim that auditability is underdeveloped?
8. Why should a finance department or computer science department care about this topic?
9. What is the minimum viable contribution for the first paper?
10. What is the 3-year publication pipeline?

## B. Reviewer #2 questions

1. Is this just another survey paper?
2. Has the field already been surveyed by Agentic Trading or other finance-agent surveys?
3. What does this review add beyond existing surveys?
4. How did you choose the included papers?
5. Why were some papers excluded?
6. Is the taxonomy original or copied from previous literature?
7. How do you avoid cherry-picking papers that support your thesis?
8. How do you define "investment research agent" precisely?
9. Why is "trustworthiness" not too broad?
10. Can your gap map be reproduced by another researcher?

## C. Methodology questions

1. What databases did you search?
2. What exact keywords did you use?
3. What date range did you include?
4. How many papers were initially found?
5. How many were screened out?
6. What were the inclusion and exclusion criteria?
7. Did you use PRISMA-style screening?
8. How did you code each paper?
9. Did more than one reviewer code the papers?
10. How did you resolve ambiguous classifications?

## D. Technical questions

1. What is an LLM agent?
2. What is the difference between a model, an agent, and a multi-agent system?
3. What is RAG?
4. What is memory in agent architecture?
5. What is reflection?
6. What is tool use?
7. What is closed-loop evaluation?
8. What is execution semantics?
9. What is transaction-cost-aware backtesting?
10. What is survivorship bias?

## E. Finance questions

1. What is the difference between stock prediction and investment research?
2. What is the buy-side research workflow?
3. Why does a real fund need risk review before portfolio action?
4. Why is transaction cost important?
5. Why is slippage important?
6. Why is turnover important?
7. Why does drawdown matter more than raw return?
8. How does regime shift affect agent evaluation?
9. What is an investment thesis?
10. What should an AI-generated investment thesis contain?

## F. Industry expert questions

1. Why would a hedge fund use this system?
2. Why would a family office pay for this?
3. What tasks can an AI agent realistically replace?
4. What tasks still require human portfolio managers?
5. How would this system reduce analyst workload?
6. How would this system reduce operational risk?
7. What is the business value of auditability?
8. What is the business value of reproducibility?
9. How would this system be integrated with Bloomberg, FactSet, or internal research systems?
10. What would be the first commercial MVP?

## G. Devil's advocate questions

1. What if LLM agents never produce reliable investment alpha?
2. What if the value is only in workflow automation, not investment performance?
3. What if auditability slows the system down too much?
4. What if human users ignore the audit trail?
5. What if agents hallucinate evidence even with RAG?
6. What if proprietary data matters more than agent architecture?
7. What if regulation prevents autonomous investment agents?
8. What if all agents converge to the same trades and create systemic risk?
9. What if memory causes anchoring and makes agents worse?
10. What if benchmark performance does not translate into real markets?

## H. First paper defense questions

1. What is the main contribution of the review paper?
2. Is the contribution the taxonomy, the database, the gap map, or the future research agenda?
3. How many papers are enough for v0.1?
4. What makes the database useful to other researchers?
5. What makes the gap map non-obvious?
6. What evidence shows that investment research agents are different from trading agents?
7. What is the strongest counterargument to the paper?
8. What would make the paper publishable?
9. Which venue would be suitable?
10. What is the next original research paper after the review?
