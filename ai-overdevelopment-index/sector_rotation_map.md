# AI Sector Rotation Map

本文件研究当第一层 AI 硬件交易过热或降温后，资金可能转向哪些行业。

## Layer 1 — AI hardware

Examples:

- GPUs
- HBM
- Foundries
- Semiconductor equipment
- Advanced packaging

Main risk:

- valuation compression
- capex slowdown
- inventory build
- crowded positioning

## Layer 2 — Physical infrastructure

Potential beneficiaries:

- power generation
- grid equipment
- transformers
- cooling systems
- data centers
- copper and electrical materials
- nuclear power and uranium

Why it matters:

Even if AI software monetization is slower than expected, physical infrastructure spending may continue because power and grid bottlenecks remain binding constraints.

## Layer 3 — AI productivity adopters

Potential beneficiaries:

- financial operations
- healthcare administration
- industrial automation
- logistics
- cybersecurity
- enterprise software
- customer service automation

Key test:

> Can these companies convert AI adoption into higher revenue per employee, lower cost per transaction, or better margins?

## Layer 4 — Defensive rotation

If AI repricing becomes a broader risk-off event, capital may rotate into:

- utilities
- consumer staples
- healthcare
- high-quality dividend stocks
- government bonds
- cash

## Rotation decision framework

| Scenario | Likely rotation |
|---|---|
| AI hardware still strong, valuations reasonable | Stay in Layer 1 selectively |
| AI capex strong but chip valuations crowded | Rotate toward Layer 2 |
| Enterprise productivity evidence improves | Add Layer 3 |
| Capex cuts and earnings downgrades spread | Move toward Layer 4 |
