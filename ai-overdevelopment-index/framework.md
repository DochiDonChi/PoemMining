# AI Overdevelopment Framework

## 1. Core distinction

我们不要问：

> AI 是不是有用？

而要问：

> 市场为 AI 付出的价格，是否已经远远超过 AI 当前和未来可验证的经济回报？

技术革命可以是真的，但投资泡沫也可以同时存在。互联网是真的，但 2000 年大量互联网股票仍然跌了 80% 以上。

## 2. Four-layer model

### Layer 1 — Capital overheating

观察 AI 投入是否过快：

- Hyperscaler AI capex growth
- GPU purchase commitments
- data-center construction pipeline
- AI startup funding
- AI-related IPO/SPAC activity
- AI company valuation expansion

如果资本开支增长远快于收入增长，风险上升。

### Layer 2 — Supply overbuild

观察供给是否开始过剩：

- GPU utilization rate
- HBM/DRAM inventory days
- data-center vacancy or under-utilization
- power connection queue saturation
- semiconductor equipment order slowdown
- cloud GPU rental price declines

如果供给继续扩张，但利用率下降，说明过度建设开始出现。

### Layer 3 — Demand validation

观察 AI 是否真的创造收入和生产率：

- AI revenue contribution
- paid enterprise AI seats
- AI usage retention
- customer ROI case studies
- labor productivity improvement
- gross margin / operating margin impact

如果企业大量投入 AI，但收入、利润率和生产率没有明显改善，估值会被重新定价。

### Layer 4 — Market mania

观察市场是否进入叙事狂热：

- AI ETF inflows
- retail trading volume in AI names
- media frequency of AI keywords
- companies adding “AI” to branding without real revenue
- valuation premium vs non-AI peers
- options call skew and leverage

当“故事”取代现金流，泡沫风险上升。

## 3. Bubble is not binary

AI 是否过度开发不是 yes/no，而是一个风险区间：

- Normal buildout: capex high, utilization high, revenue accelerating
- Healthy correction: valuation falls but revenue remains strong
- Overheating: capex accelerates faster than revenue
- Bubble: supply rises, utilization falls, revenue disappoints, valuation still high
- Bust: capex cuts, inventory rises, earnings are downgraded, financing tightens

## 4. The main equation

```text
AI Overdevelopment Risk = Investment Growth - Verified Economic Return
```

其中 Verified Economic Return 包括：

- incremental revenue
- margin improvement
- productivity gains
- free cash flow
- customer retention

## 5. Early-warning hierarchy

最早的信号通常不是股价，而是：

1. order lead-time shortening
2. cloud GPU rental price falling
3. hyperscaler capex guidance flattening
4. HBM inventory rising
5. data-center power demand forecast cuts
6. semiconductor equipment orders slowing
7. AI ETF flow reversal
8. earnings revisions turning negative

## 6. Application to Japan / Korea / US

### US

Focus: hyperscaler capex, AI software monetization, NVIDIA/semiconductor valuation.

### Korea

Focus: Samsung, SK Hynix, HBM pricing, memory cycle, export data.

### Japan

Focus: Tokyo Electron, Advantest, SoftBank, industrial automation, semiconductor equipment, foreign flows.
