## 一、开市前结论｜Pre-Open Conclusion

### 中文

今日对日经225的基准判断为**偏多开局、盘中高波动、上涨空间受日元与日债限制**。隔夜美股继续追逐AI、HBM与存储芯片受益者，Nvidia上涨约4%，SK Hynix美国存托凭证上市首日上涨约13%，强化日本半导体设备、测试和AI供应链的正面映射。同时，日本政府推动GPIF及国内长期资金增加日本资产配置，已带动日元、日债与日股同步改善，形成短线政策托底。

不过，风险并没有消失。日本10年期国债收益率在7月9日一度升至2.90%的30年高位，随后因政策表态回落至约2.77%；USD/JPY从162上方回落至约161.36，说明市场正在提高对“隐性干预”或实际资金回流的定价。若日元快速升值，出口股和日经权重股的盈利折算优势将下降；若日债收益率重新冲击2.90%，高估值科技股可能承压。

**日经225今日预期：**

- 基准情景（55%）：高开或平高开后震荡，日内约 **+0.4%至+1.3%**。
- 强势情景（20%）：半导体权重股全面跟随美股上涨，指数上试 **+1.5%至+2.2%**。
- 风险情景（25%）：日元快速升破160.8、JGB收益率反弹或油价上升，指数回吐至 **-0.8%至-1.5%**。

关键判断不是“AI情绪是否强”，而是**AI情绪能否抵消日元升值与日本利率上行**。

### English

Our base case is for a **constructive open followed by elevated intraday volatility, with upside capped by yen appreciation and high JGB yields**. U.S. investors continued to favor AI, HBM and memory-chip beneficiaries on Friday. Nvidia gained roughly 4%, while SK Hynix’s U.S.-listed depositary receipts rose about 13% on debut, providing a positive read-through for Japanese semiconductor equipment, testing and AI supply-chain names. In parallel, Tokyo’s push for GPIF and other long-term domestic institutions to allocate more capital to Japanese assets has supported the yen, JGBs and equities, creating a near-term policy backstop.

The risk backdrop remains material. Japan’s 10-year government bond yield reached a 30-year high of 2.90% on July 9 before easing toward 2.77% after policy signals. USD/JPY also retreated from above 162 to around 161.36, indicating that markets are assigning a higher probability to “stealth intervention” or genuine domestic capital repatriation. A rapid yen rally would erode the translation benefit enjoyed by exporters and index heavyweights, while a renewed move in the 10-year JGB yield toward 2.90% would pressure long-duration technology valuations.

**Nikkei 225 expectation for today:**

- Base case (55%): flat-to-higher open followed by consolidation, approximately **+0.4% to +1.3%** intraday.
- Bull case (20%): broad semiconductor follow-through, allowing the index to test **+1.5% to +2.2%**.
- Risk case (25%): a sharp yen move through 160.8, renewed JGB selling or higher oil prices could drive a reversal toward **-0.8% to -1.5%**.

The key question is not whether AI sentiment is strong; it is whether **AI momentum can offset a stronger yen and higher Japanese discount rates**.

## 三、核心市场信号｜Core Market Signals

### 中文

**1. 美股AI与SOX链条：偏多，但分化明显。** 7月10日标普500上涨约0.42%，纳斯达克上涨约0.29%，Nvidia上涨约4%，SK Hynix美国存托凭证上涨约13%。本周市场将重点等待TSMC财报，因其收入指引、先进制程需求及定价将直接验证AI硬件需求是否继续超预期。SOX此前曾出现单日4%以上下跌，说明板块已经进入高波动、高分化阶段。

**2. 韩国半导体：继续作为AI周期领先指标。** SK Hynix大型美国融资成功、股价强势，反映市场仍愿意为HBM和存储稀缺性支付溢价。韩国存储链当前给出的信号仍是需求强，而不是库存恶化；但资本市场融资规模上升也意味着未来供给扩张风险加大。

**3. USD/JPY：从单向利好转为双刃剑。** 美元兑日元近期一度升至约162.40，随后因日本推动本土资金配置而回落至约161.36。弱日元仍支持出口商利润，但当汇率进入政策敏感区后，进一步贬值可能触发官方干预；相反，快速升值又会打压出口和日经权重股。

**4. 日本国债：仍是最大宏观风险。** 10年期JGB收益率7月9日触及2.90%，为30年高位；政策表态后回落至约2.77%。收益率回落有利于科技股估值，但目前仍远高于过去长期区间，说明日本财政、通胀和货币政策风险尚未解除。

**5. 资金流向：政策回流预期升温，但真实流量尚未完全确认。** 截至7月4日当周，日本投资者净买入约8,245亿日元海外股票，为三个月最大规模，构成日元贬值压力。政府随后推动GPIF和国内长期资金增加日本资产配置。市场已先行交易这一预期，但要确认趋势，需要看到后续官方资金流数据持续转向。

### English

**1. U.S. AI and SOX complex: constructive, but increasingly bifurcated.** On July 10, the S&P 500 rose about 0.42%, the Nasdaq gained roughly 0.29%, Nvidia advanced around 4%, and SK Hynix’s U.S.-listed depositary receipts climbed approximately 13%. TSMC’s upcoming earnings will be the central validation event for AI hardware demand, particularly its revenue outlook, advanced-node utilization and pricing. The SOX index has also recently suffered a one-day decline of more than 4%, confirming that the sector has entered a high-volatility, high-dispersion regime.

**2. Korean semiconductors remain a leading indicator for the AI cycle.** SK Hynix’s successful U.S. capital raise and strong trading debut show that investors are still willing to pay a scarcity premium for HBM and advanced memory. The Korean memory chain currently signals strong demand rather than inventory deterioration. However, the rising scale of capital formation also increases the risk of future supply expansion.

**3. USD/JPY has shifted from a one-way equity tailwind to a two-sided risk.** The pair recently traded near 162.40 before easing toward 161.36 after Japan signaled support for domestic asset allocation. A weak yen still benefits exporters, but once the exchange rate enters a politically sensitive zone, further depreciation raises intervention risk. Conversely, a rapid yen rally would pressure exporters and Nikkei heavyweights.

**4. JGBs remain the largest macro risk.** The 10-year JGB yield touched 2.90% on July 9, its highest level in 30 years, before declining toward 2.77% following policy comments. Lower yields support technology valuations, but the current level remains far above Japan’s historical norm, indicating that fiscal, inflation and monetary-policy risks are unresolved.

**5. Capital flows: expectations of domestic repatriation are rising, but the actual flow reversal is not yet confirmed.** Japanese investors purchased approximately ¥824.5 billion of foreign equities in the week ended July 4, the largest amount in three months, adding pressure on the yen. The government subsequently encouraged GPIF and other long-term domestic investors to increase allocations to Japanese assets. Markets have already priced part of this policy signal; confirmation requires subsequent official flow data to show a sustained turn.

## 四、领先指标｜Leading Indicators

### 中文

1. **TSMC二季度业绩与指引：** 若上调收入、先进制程利用率或价格预期，支持AI硬件需求继续扩张；若仅维持指引而资本开支继续提高，则供给过剩风险上升。
2. **USD/JPY 160.8与162.5区间：** 跌破160.8代表日元快速升值，可能压制出口股；突破162.5则提高官方干预概率。
3. **日本10年期国债收益率2.75%与2.90%：** 稳定在2.75%以下有利于估值扩张；重回2.90%上方将显著提高日经风险分数。
4. **韩国存储股相对SOX表现：** SK Hynix、Samsung与Micron若继续跑赢，代表AI需求正在由GPU扩散到存储；若存储股先于Nvidia转弱，可能是周期拐点。
5. **日本外资净买入与国内资金回流数据：** 政策表态必须转化为真实资金流，才能形成持续支撑。

### English

1. **TSMC’s second-quarter results and guidance:** An upgrade to revenue, advanced-node utilization or pricing would support continued AI hardware expansion. Unchanged demand guidance alongside higher capital expenditure would instead raise overcapacity risk.
2. **USD/JPY at 160.8 and 162.5:** A break below 160.8 would signal rapid yen appreciation and pressure exporters. A move above 162.5 would materially increase the probability of official intervention.
3. **The 10-year JGB yield at 2.75% and 2.90%:** Stability below 2.75% would support valuation expansion. A return above 2.90% would materially increase the Nikkei Risk Score.
4. **Korean memory equities relative to SOX:** Continued outperformance by SK Hynix, Samsung and Micron would indicate that AI demand is broadening from GPUs into memory. If memory stocks weaken before Nvidia, it may be an early cycle-turn signal.
5. **Foreign inflows into Japan and domestic repatriation data:** Policy language must translate into measurable capital flows before it can provide durable support.

## 五、反方观点｜Counterarguments

### 中文

**反方观点一：日经可能明显强于预期。** 如果日本政府推动GPIF和长期资金配置日本资产被视为真正的结构性政策，而不是口头干预，外资可能重新提高日本股票权重。配合美股AI上涨，日经可能突破基准区间。

**反方观点二：高JGB收益率未必立即打压股市。** 如果收益率上升反映名义增长、工资和通胀改善，而不是财政失控，银行、保险和价值股可能受益，并抵消科技股压力。

**反方观点三：AI资本投入并未过剩。** Micron、SK Hynix和TSMC的扩产可能只是填补长期结构性短缺。若HBM、先进封装和数据中心电力约束持续，当前高资本开支仍可能获得足够回报。

### English

**Counterargument 1: The Nikkei could materially outperform our range.** If the government’s effort to redirect GPIF and long-term domestic capital into Japanese assets is viewed as a genuine structural policy rather than verbal intervention, foreign investors may raise Japan allocations. Combined with stronger U.S. AI equities, this could push the Nikkei above our base-case range.

**Counterargument 2: High JGB yields may not immediately damage equities.** If rising yields reflect stronger nominal growth, wages and inflation rather than fiscal instability, banks, insurers and value stocks could benefit enough to offset pressure on technology shares.

**Counterargument 3: AI capital expenditure may not be excessive.** Expansion by Micron, SK Hynix and TSMC may simply be addressing a persistent structural shortage. If HBM, advanced packaging and data-centre power constraints remain binding, current investment levels could still generate adequate returns.

## 六、证伪条件｜Falsification Conditions

### 中文

本报告的偏多基准判断将在以下情况下被证伪：

- 日经开盘后无法守住前一交易日收盘附近，并在半导体股上涨时仍持续下跌。
- USD/JPY快速跌破160.8，同时出口股明显跑输银行与内需股。
- 10年期JGB收益率重新升破2.90%，且收益率曲线进一步陡峭化。
- 日本半导体设备、测试和存储相关股票未能跟随Nvidia、SK Hynix和Micron的隔夜表现。
- 油价因美伊冲突显著上升，市场重新交易输入型通胀和日本贸易条件恶化。

### English

Our constructive base case would be falsified under the following conditions:

- The Nikkei fails to hold near the previous close after the open and continues to decline despite strength in semiconductor shares.
- USD/JPY falls rapidly below 160.8, while exporters materially underperform banks and domestic-demand stocks.
- The 10-year JGB yield moves back above 2.90% and the yield curve steepens further.
- Japanese semiconductor equipment, testing and memory-related names fail to follow the overnight strength in Nvidia, SK Hynix and Micron.
- Oil prices rise sharply on renewed U.S.-Iran escalation, reviving concerns over imported inflation and Japan’s terms of trade.

## 七、当日三个重点观察｜Three Key Observations for Today

### 中文

**第一，观察日经上涨是否由半导体权重股主导。** 若Advantest、Tokyo Electron、SoftBank Group等高权重股票同时走强，指数上涨质量较高；若指数上涨主要来自政策相关金融和价值股，则AI映射弱于表面。

**第二，观察USD/JPY与日经是否出现负相关反转。** 弱日元过去通常支持日经，但在162附近，市场可能更担心干预。若日元升值而日经仍上涨，说明国内资金回流和AI动能足以抵消汇率压力。

**第三，观察10年期JGB能否保持在2.80%以下。** 这是今天风险资产能否维持估值扩张的最重要宏观条件。若收益率重新快速上升，指数高开后回落的概率显著增加。

### English

**First, determine whether semiconductor heavyweights are driving the advance.** If Advantest, Tokyo Electron and SoftBank Group rise together, the quality of the index move will be stronger. If gains are concentrated in policy-sensitive financials and value stocks, the AI read-through will be weaker than the headline index suggests.

**Second, watch for a reversal in the usual relationship between USD/JPY and the Nikkei.** Yen weakness has historically supported Japanese equities, but near 162 the market may focus more on intervention risk. If the yen strengthens while the Nikkei continues to rise, domestic capital support and AI momentum are likely strong enough to absorb currency pressure.

**Third, monitor whether the 10-year JGB yield remains below 2.80%.** This is the most important macro condition for sustaining valuation expansion today. A rapid renewed increase in yields would materially raise the probability of an opening rally fading.
