# Bilingual Reporting Standard｜中英文双语报告规范

所有自动生成的日报、周报、月报和专题研究必须采用中英文对照格式，并遵守以下规则。

All automated daily, weekly, monthly, and thematic research reports must use a bilingual Chinese-English format and follow the rules below.

## 1. Language order｜语言顺序

每个章节先写中文，再写英文。英文不是逐字直译，而是专业、自然、适合投资研究场景的表达。

Each section must present Chinese first, followed by English. The English version should not be a literal translation; it should read naturally and professionally in an investment-research context.

## 2. Required structure｜固定结构

1. Executive Summary｜管理层摘要
2. What Happened｜发生了什么
3. Why It Matters｜为什么重要
4. Market Consensus｜市场共识
5. Our View｜我们的观点
6. Evidence｜证据
7. Counterarguments｜反方观点
8. Leading Indicators｜领先指标
9. Risk Score｜风险评分
10. Scenario Analysis｜情景分析
11. Investment Implications｜投资启示
12. What Would Prove Us Wrong｜证伪条件
13. What to Watch Next｜下一步观察
14. Sources｜资料来源

## 3. Formatting example｜格式示例

### Executive Summary｜管理层摘要

**中文**

日经指数本周回落，主要受AI估值重定价、外资获利了结和美元兑日元波动影响。目前更接近估值调整，而不是基本面崩坏。

**English**

The Nikkei declined this week, mainly due to AI valuation repricing, foreign profit-taking, and volatility in USD/JPY. At this stage, the move looks more like a valuation correction than a fundamental breakdown.

## 4. Tables｜表格

表格应尽量双语化，避免同一信息重复两遍。

Tables should be bilingual where possible and avoid unnecessary duplication.

| 指标 Indicator | 当前状态 Current Status | 解释 Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| AI CapEx | 偏高 Elevated | 投资增速仍快于可验证收入增长 Investment growth still exceeds verified revenue growth |

## 5. Evidence rules｜证据规则

- 所有时效性数据必须标明日期。
- 所有新闻和数据来源必须列出。
- 区分事实、推断和观点。
- 不允许把市场传闻当成已确认事实。
- 数据不可得时必须明确说明。

- Every time-sensitive data point must include a date.
- All news and data sources must be listed.
- Facts, inference, and opinion must be clearly separated.
- Market rumors must not be presented as confirmed facts.
- Missing or unavailable data must be disclosed explicitly.

## 6. Risk scores｜风险评分

每份报告至少包含：

- AI Cycle Risk Score｜AI周期风险评分
- Nikkei Risk Score｜日经风险评分
- Confidence Level｜判断信心度
- Change vs Previous Report｜较上期变化

## 7. File naming｜文件命名

- Daily: `daily/YYYY-MM-DD-morning.md`, `daily/YYYY-MM-DD-close.md`
- Weekly: `weekly/YYYY-Www-investment-committee.md`
- Consensus: `weekly/YYYY-Www-consensus-vs-our-view.md`
- Monthly: `monthly/YYYY-MM-topic-slug.md`
- PDF output: `pdf/YYYY-Www-investment-committee.pdf`
