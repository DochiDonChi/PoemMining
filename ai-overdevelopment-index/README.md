# AI Overdevelopment Index

本项目用于系统研究一个核心问题：**AI 是否已经被过度开发，临界点在哪里，哪些数据可以提前预警泡沫或产能过剩？**

This project studies whether AI investment has moved ahead of real economic returns, and builds a practical dashboard for identifying early warning signals before the market fully prices them in.

## Core thesis

AI 技术本身可能是真实的长期革命，但 AI 相关资产价格、资本开支、数据中心建设、GPU 需求和企业估值，可能在不同阶段出现过度开发。我们不把问题简化成“AI 是真还是假”，而是量化：

> AI Investment vs AI Economic Return

当 AI 资本投入、基础设施建设和市场估值明显快于实际收入、生产率和现金流改善时，系统进入过热区。

## Main research questions

1. AI 投入是否已经超过实际经济回报？
2. 哪些指标能比股价更早预警 AI 周期转弱？
3. 数据中心、GPU、HBM、半导体设备是否出现供给过剩？
4. AI 是否真正提高了企业劳动生产率和利润率？
5. 市场现在交易的是真实盈利，还是远期叙事？
6. 如果 AI 基础设施第一层降温，资金会轮动到哪些第二层或第三层资产？

## Project modules

- `framework.md` — AI 过度开发判断框架
- `indicators.yml` — 指标清单、权重、临界点
- `data_sources.md` — 可追踪数据源
- `research_plan.md` — 研究路线图

## Working definition

AI overdevelopment begins when:

1. AI capex keeps accelerating;
2. AI revenue and productivity gains lag behind;
3. GPU/data-center utilization weakens;
4. free cash flow deteriorates;
5. valuation remains priced for perfection;
6. market positioning is crowded.

## Risk score concept

The project will eventually produce an **AI Cycle Risk Score** from 0 to 100:

- 0–30: normal expansion
- 31–50: watch zone
- 51–70: overheating
- 71–85: bubble risk
- 86–100: high probability of sharp repricing

This is not financial advice. It is a research and decision-support framework.
