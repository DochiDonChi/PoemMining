# Research Plan

## Phase 1 — Define the framework

Objective: turn the discussion into a repeatable research process.

Deliverables:

- Define AI overdevelopment
- Define AI Cycle Risk Score
- Define risk bands and key indicators
- Build source map

Status: initial draft created.

## Phase 2 — Build the first manual dashboard

Objective: manually update indicators once per week.

Tasks:

1. Create a weekly scorecard template.
2. Track 15–20 indicators.
3. Assign green/yellow/red status.
4. Produce a weekly one-page memo.

Suggested output format:

```text
AI Cycle Risk Score: 62/100
Risk band: Overheating
Main driver: AI capex growth is still high, but semiconductor orders and market breadth are weakening.
Action: reduce first-layer AI exposure; monitor second-layer infrastructure rotation.
```

- Nikkei 225

Questions:

3. Does SOX lead Nikkei semiconductor names?
4. Does USD/JPY amplify or offset Japan equity risk?
5. Are foreign flows a leading or lagging indicator?

## Phase 4 — Early warning model

Objective: build a practical warning system.

Potential signals:

- SOX breaks key moving averages
- AI ETF outflows accelerate
- hyperscaler capex guidance slows
- HBM pricing weakens
- cloud GPU rental price falls
- semiconductor equipment book-to-bill falls below 1
- foreign flows out of Japan/Korea turn negative
- options put/call ratio rises
- market breadth deteriorates

Output:

- Low risk
- Watch
- Reduce risk
- Hedge
- Capital preservation

## Phase 6 — Investment committee style memos

Each memo should answer:

1. What happened?
2. Why did it happen?
3. Is it priced in?
4. Who wins?
5. Who loses?
6. What would prove the thesis wrong?
7. What should we monitor next?
