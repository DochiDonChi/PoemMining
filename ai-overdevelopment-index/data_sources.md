# Data Sources

This file maps each research module to potential public or semi-public data sources.

## 1. Capital overheating

### Hyperscaler capex

Potential sources:

- Company earnings reports: Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Oracle
- 10-Q / 10-K filings
- Earnings call transcripts
- Investor presentations
- Bloomberg / FactSet / Visible Alpha if available

Key fields:

- capital expenditures
- data-center capex
- AI infrastructure commentary
- depreciation schedule
- cloud revenue growth
- AI product revenue disclosures

## 7. Research discipline

Every data point should be classified into one of three groups:

1. Leading indicator — appears before earnings or price decline
2. Coincident indicator — moves with the market
3. Lagging indicator — confirms after price has moved

The project should prioritize leading indicators.
