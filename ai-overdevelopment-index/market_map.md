# Market Linkage Map

本文件用于把 AI 周期风险与可交易市场连接起来，判断哪个市场先反应、哪个市场滞后，以及风险如何跨市场传导。

## Core transmission chain

```text
Hyperscaler AI CapEx
        ↓
GPU / HBM / Foundry Demand
        ↓
US Semiconductors / Korea Memory / Japan Equipment
        ↓
SOX / KOSPI / Nikkei 225
        ↓
Foreign Flows / FX / Bond Yields
        ↓
Broader Risk Appetite
```

## United States

Primary indicators:

- S&P 500 concentration
- Nasdaq 100 breadth
- SOX trend
- NVIDIA / Broadcom / Micron / AMD
- Hyperscaler capex guidance
- US 10-year Treasury yield
- AI ETF flows
- Options skew and put/call ratio

Key question:

> Is the US market broadening beyond AI leaders, or are index highs increasingly dependent on a narrow group of companies?

## Japan

Primary indicators:

- Nikkei 225 and TOPIX divergence
- Tokyo Electron
- Advantest
- SoftBank Group
- Kioxia
- USD/JPY
- JGB 10-year and 30-year yields
- Weekly foreign investor flow
- Market breadth

Key question:

> Is Japan being supported by broad earnings and governance reform, or mainly by weak yen and AI-linked index heavyweights?

## Korea

Primary indicators:

- KOSPI and KOSDAQ
- Samsung Electronics
- SK Hynix
- KRW/USD
- Semiconductor export growth
- HBM pricing
- Daily foreign net buying/selling

Key question:

> Does Korea lead the global AI hardware cycle because memory prices and foreign flows turn before Japan and the US?

## Cross-market lead/lag hypotheses

1. Korea memory and exports may lead Japan equipment stocks.
2. SOX may lead Nikkei semiconductor names.
3. USD/JPY may amplify Nikkei upside, but sharp yen appreciation can accelerate drawdowns.
4. JGB yield spikes may trigger valuation compression even when earnings remain strong.
5. Foreign flows may be a coincident indicator during normal periods but a leading indicator during crowded positioning reversals.
